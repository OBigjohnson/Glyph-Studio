# Manual bank transfer and finance

GLYPH records invoices, line items, expenses, manual transfer payments, receipts,
and payment status in Supabase. Invoices created with a client selected are
shared automatically to that client's in-app portal. Studio owners can still
change sharing from Finance; clients see only invoices shared with their client
record, can print them, and can view the studio's bank details when they need
to pay by transfer. Payment details are fetched only for the authenticated
client's own shared invoice. Sharing does not send an email or mark an invoice
paid. A receipt can be generated by the studio after a bank transfer is
verified; the browser can then print that receipt for the studio and the client.
Previously created invoices linked to a client are also shared by the invoice
visibility migration. A confirmed portal account is linked to client records
only when its email matches the record's email.

The current workflow keeps things simple and reliable without a checkout gateway:

1. In Studio Settings, the owner stores the bank name, account name, account
   number, and a reference note.
2. In Finance, the studio creates an invoice with the client selected. The
   invoice is automatically shared to that client's portal. Existing invoices
   can still be shared or hidden from the portal in Finance.
3. The client opens the invoice in the portal, clicks Pay invoice, and sees the
   studio's transfer instructions with the invoice number as the reference.
4. After the studio verifies the transfer, it records the payment in Finance.
   This creates a payment record and generates a receipt automatically.
5. Receipts are retained in **Finance → Receipts**. The studio can print its
   own receipt, print a separate client copy, or copy a client portal link that
   opens that specific receipt. The studio copy identifies the studio as the
   receiver and says **Payment receipt / Amount received**; the client copy
   identifies the client as the payer and the studio as the payee, and says
   **Payment confirmation / Amount you paid**.
6. The invoice and receipt are then visible in the studio and client portal,
   and the payment contributes to the studio's recorded income.

This avoids a payment gateway and keeps the payment confirmation under the
studio's control. Receipts are only issued after a manual verification step,
so a printed invoice is still a request for payment rather than proof of payment.
Sharing a receipt makes it available in the client's authenticated portal; GLYPH
does not email the receipt or send a message on the studio's behalf.
