# Importing and publishing portfolio work

1. Create your GLYPH studio account at `/create-account` and sign in.
   Signup creates your private workspace automatically.
2. Open **Studio → Portfolio → Import folder** and select the
   `C:\Users\USER\Pictures\PORTFOLIO` folder (or another folder).
3. GLYPH imports JPG and PNG artwork and PDF files as separate, unpublished
   drafts. Filenames become starting titles; files remain private until you
   publish a story.
4. Edit titles, add a service and project story, and select related private
   drafts to bring their images/PDFs together under one case study.
5. Publish reviewed case studies to make their images and PDF links available
   on the public portfolio. Unpublish a case study to make the files private
   again.

Files are uploaded sequentially. Resumable uploads are used for files larger
than 6 MB. JPG, PNG, and PDF are supported up to 50 MB per file. Keep this page
open until the upload finishes; failed files are reported and may be retried.
Never include client-confidential work in a public case study unless you have
permission to publish it.
